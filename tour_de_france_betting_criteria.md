# Tour de France Betting Evaluation Criteria

This document outlines the essential criteria for analyzing and evaluating potential wagers on the Tour de France.

## 1. Rider Profile & Specialization
* **General Classification (GC) Contenders:** High aerobic capacity, exceptional climbing ability, and strong time-trialing skills.
* **Sprinters:** High peak power output, bike-handling skills in chaotic bunch finishes, and strong team lead-out trains.
* **Climbers / Baroudeurs:** High power-to-weight ratio (W/kg) for long, steep ascents; willingness to join long-range breakaways.
* **Time-Trial Specialists:** Superior aerodynamics, steady high-wattage pacing, and technical cornering ability.

## 2. Stage Terrain & Route Analysis
* **Flat Stages:** High probability of a bunch sprint finish; favor teams with dedicated lead-out trains.
* **Mountain / Hill Stages:** Higher likelihood of GC shakeups or successful breakaways; monitor total vertical gain and summit finishes.
* **Individual Time Trials (ITT):** Strictly tests individual aerodynamic efficiency and steady-state power output.
* **Weather Conditions:** Evaluate crosswinds (potential for echelons), extreme heat, or wet descents which heavily impact safety and gaps.

## 3. Team Strength & Tactical Roles
* **Domestique Support:** A GC leader requires strong climbers to pace them in the mountains and flat-land engines to shield them from wind.
* **Split Priorities:** Assess if a team is divided between hunting stage wins with a sprinter and protecting a GC contender, which dilutes support.
* **Team Synergy:** Historical cooperation, lead-out positioning efficiency, and tactical management reputation.

## 4. Form, Fatigue & Historical Performance
* **Recent Race Outcomes:** Assess performance in key preparation races like the Critérium du Dauphiné or Tour de Suisse.
* **Historical Consistency:** Past Grand Tour finishes, resilience over a three-week race, and recovery capabilities.
* **Injury & Recovery:** Recent crashes, illnesses, or forced training breaks prior to the Grand Départ.
